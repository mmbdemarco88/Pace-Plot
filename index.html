Official Safety Protocol

Pace Plot Safety Agreement

Pedestrian right-of-way, road rules & runner awareness

Pedestrian Signals & Right-of-Way

Pace Plot loops prioritize lit sidewalks and quiet paths, but runners must always obey street signals, crosswalk rules, and yield to vehicular traffic.

Situational Hearing & Ambient Audio

Runners are strongly encouraged to use bone-conduction headphones, ambient passthrough mode, or keep one ear open so emergency sirens and approaching vehicles remain audible.

Low-Light & Night Visibility

Wear retroreflective apparel, clip-on safety strobes, or headlamps when running before dawn, at dusk, or after dark along roadway shoulders.

SafeGuard Companion Disclaimer

SafeGuard live tracking and fall detection are assistive safety companion tools. They do not replace certified 911 local emergency response dispatch services.
